Ready Set Solutions Privacy Policy

Pilot policy — updated August 8, 2026

Ready Set Solutions and ComplianceBinder help customers organize inspection-readiness tasks, evidence, corrective actions, reminders, and reports. This policy describes the information handled by the application during the pilot stage.

Information collected

The application may store account email addresses, password hashes, facility or binder names, task and readiness-item details, uploaded document metadata and files, billing/subscription status received from the payment provider, reminder activity, and security or operational logs needed to run and protect the service.

Assisted-living pilot data minimization

The initial assisted-living pilot is designed to minimize intentional collection of resident clinical records and protected health information. Customers and Ready Set field personnel should not upload or photograph residents, medication information, resident-identifying records, medical charts, financial information, or other sensitive information unless that information is specifically authorized and the deployment, agreements, and security controls are configured for that data type.

Evidence and uploaded documents

Users control what they upload and are responsible for having authority to provide it. Evidence should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the agreed readiness scope. When possible, use facility-level, de-identified, or redacted evidence rather than sensitive source records.

Payments

Payment processing is handled through Stripe when configured by the operator. Ready Set Solutions stores plan and subscription-status metadata needed to control paid features but does not store full payment-card numbers in ComplianceBinder.

How information is used

Information is used to provide facility/binder organization, task and corrective-action tracking, evidence storage, report generation, billing access, reminders, customer support, security monitoring, and service administration.

Data retention and backups

Data may be retained while an account or service relationship remains active, subject to the applicable customer agreement, deletion request, legal obligation, or operational backup cycle. Customers should retain independent copies of records that are legally or operationally critical and should not rely on Ready Set Solutions as the only repository for required business records.

Security

The application uses controls including password hashing, authenticated access, upload restrictions, report escaping, and production configuration requirements. Production deployments should also use encrypted transport, managed credentials, durable storage, backups, and access controls appropriate to the agreed customer data scope. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Customer and field-personnel responsibilities

Customers and Ready Set personnel must avoid unnecessary collection of personal information, follow the authorized walkthrough/data scope, and promptly report accidental capture or disclosure of sensitive information so it can be reviewed and handled appropriately.

Pilot status

This is a pilot-stage privacy policy. The service, contracts, security architecture, and privacy documentation should receive qualified legal and security review before broad commercial rollout involving routine protected health information or other highly sensitive regulated data.

Contact

Contact the operator of your Ready Set Solutions deployment for privacy, access, deletion, support, or security questions.
