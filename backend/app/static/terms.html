Ready Set Solutions Terms of Use

Pilot terms — updated August 8, 2026

Purpose

Ready Set Solutions and ComplianceBinder provide inspection-readiness, documentation, task, evidence, and reporting tools and services. Ready Set Solutions is an independent private service and is not the Arizona Department of Health Services or another regulator.

No official inspection, certification, or guarantee

A Ready Set walkthrough or report is not an official regulatory inspection, certification, legal opinion, accreditation, or guarantee of compliance. Ready Set Solutions does not warrant a deficiency-free survey, continued licensure, avoidance of enforcement action, or any particular regulatory outcome. Facility owners and licensees remain responsible for determining and maintaining compliance with applicable requirements.

No legal or regulatory advice

Templates, checklists, citations, summaries, reminders, measurements, findings, and corrective-action suggestions are provided to assist readiness and organization. Users remain responsible for verifying current requirements using official authorities and appropriate professional advisors. If a Ready Set summary differs from an official statute, rule, agency interpretation, or order, the official authority controls.

Regulatory changes

Laws, rules, agency guidance, interpretations, and facility circumstances can change. Regulatory references reflect the information reviewed for a particular workflow or report date and may require re-verification before later use.

Measurements and future LiDAR features

Mobile-device LiDAR, computer vision, and similar technologies are screening and documentation aids and may contain measurement error. A screening measurement should not be treated as a final threshold-dependent regulatory conclusion when measurement uncertainty could change the finding. Users and Ready Set personnel must follow the approved verification procedure for legally material measurements.

Privacy and authorized data

Users are responsible for the accuracy, legality, authorization, and appropriateness of information they upload. The initial Ready Set assisted-living pilot is intended to minimize collection of resident clinical information. Users and field personnel should avoid uploading or photographing residents, medication information, resident-identifying records, or other sensitive information unless such information is specifically authorized and the deployment is configured and contractually approved for that data.

Evidence and photographs

Evidence should be relevant to the readiness condition being documented and should avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive information. Customers are responsible for obtaining any permissions required for access to the facility and for information they provide to Ready Set Solutions.

Customer responsibility for corrective actions

Ready Set Solutions may organize or suggest corrective-action steps, but facility management is responsible for deciding what action is appropriate, obtaining professional advice when needed, assigning responsible personnel, completing corrections, and verifying ongoing compliance.

Billing

Paid features may require an active or trialing plan. Payment processing is handled through Stripe when configured by the operator. One-time pilot/setup services and recurring subscriptions are separate commercial offerings. Refunds, cancellations, and service scope are subject to the applicable order, proposal, or published operator policy.

Availability and record retention

The service is provided without a guarantee of uninterrupted operation. Customers should retain independent copies of critical business and regulatory records and should not use Ready Set Solutions as the sole repository for records they are legally required to preserve.

Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Ready Set Solutions is not responsible for official inspection outcomes, regulatory decisions or penalties, inaccurate or incomplete information supplied by users, changes in law or agency interpretation after a report date, or business losses resulting from reliance on incomplete or inaccurate records or unverified conclusions.

Pilot status and legal review

These terms are practical pilot-stage operating terms and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before broad commercial rollout or before expanding the service to routinely handle protected health information or other highly sensitive regulated data.

Contact

Contact the operator of your Ready Set Solutions deployment for support, billing, privacy, or terms questions.
