# Ready Set Solutions — Pilot Sales Playbook

## Sales objective

Sell the **$299 Ready Set Pilot** to Arizona assisted-living operators, prove the readiness outcome is valuable, then convert useful pilots to recurring software.

The first commercial milestone is **3 paid pilots**, not a large sales organization.

## What we sell

Do not lead with software features.

Lead with the outcome:

> Ready Set Solutions helps Arizona assisted-living operators find inspection-readiness gaps before the next survey and organize corrective evidence in one report.

The customer can use the software themselves, or a Ready Set Readiness Specialist can support the walkthrough and reporting process.

## What we never say

Never claim:

- "We guarantee you will pass."
- "We certify your facility compliant."
- "This is the same as an ADHS inspection."
- "ADHS approved us."
- "This will prevent all fines."
- "This item is definitely a violation" unless a qualified reviewer has verified both the authority and evidence and the wording is appropriate.

## Ideal customer profile

Prioritize:

1. Owner/operators with 2–10 Arizona assisted-living homes
2. Independent homes with direct access to the owner or certified manager
3. Facilities preparing for survey activity or operational changes
4. Operators still relying heavily on paper binders/spreadsheets
5. Facilities where management wants an independent second set of eyes

De-prioritize initially:

- large national senior-living chains
- customers demanding EHR/eMAR replacement
- prospects demanding a guaranteed inspection result
- facilities expecting Ready Set to become their legal counsel or official compliance officer

## Opening call script

> Hi [Name], this is [Rep] with Ready Set Solutions. We help Arizona assisted-living operators find inspection-readiness gaps before the next survey and organize the corrective evidence in one clear report. Your team can use the software themselves, or a Ready Set Readiness Specialist can support the walkthrough. We are not ADHS and this is not an official inspection. We are opening a small paid pilot group at $299 per facility. Could I show you the workflow and sample report in about 15 minutes?

## 15-minute fit call

### Minute 1–3: identify pain

Ask:

- How many assisted-living locations do you operate?
- Who currently owns survey/readiness preparation?
- What is hardest to keep organized before a survey?
- Are documents, physical walkthroughs or corrective actions the bigger problem?
- How do you currently track open readiness items?

### Minute 4–7: show the outcome

Show:

- facility readiness binder
- assisted-living readiness checklist
- evidence upload
- open-item tracking
- final readiness report

Do not spend the call explaining architecture, frameworks or LiDAR plans.

### Minute 8–10: explain the two delivery modes

**DIY:** their team uses Ready Set.

**Done-with-you:** a Ready Set Readiness Specialist supports the walkthrough/evidence/report workflow.

### Minute 11–13: qualify urgency and authority

Ask:

- Is there a particular inspection/readiness concern driving this now?
- If this appears useful, who approves a $299 pilot?
- Would you prefer your staff to work the checklist or have field support?
- If the pilot produces a useful report, would ongoing reminders/reporting be valuable?

### Minute 14–15: close

> The pilot is $299 for one facility. We set up the readiness binder, work through the initial readiness process, organize the report, and give you the working account. If the report is useful, you can continue with the recurring software. Should we start with [facility name]?

## Common objections

### "We already have software."

> That makes sense. Ready Set is not asking you to replace your resident-care system. We focus on whether the facility can identify, document and close inspection-readiness gaps before an official survey, including the physical walkthrough and supporting evidence.

### "We already have binders."

> Good. The question is whether the right evidence is current, easy to retrieve, and connected to open corrective actions. Ready Set can sit alongside the system you already use rather than forcing a full replacement.

### "I do not have time for another program."

> That is why the pilot has a done-with-you option. Your staff can use it, or a Ready Set Readiness Specialist can support the walkthrough and deliver the organized report.

### "Can you guarantee I will pass?"

> No. Ready Set is not the regulator and cannot guarantee an inspection outcome. We identify and organize readiness issues so management can decide what needs correction before an official survey.

### "$299 is too much."

> The pilot is designed to answer whether this saves management time and exposes useful readiness gaps before you commit to anything larger. If it does not produce a useful actionable report, ongoing software would not make sense for either side.

### "Just send me information."

> I can do that. The most useful thing to review is the sample readiness report because that shows the actual deliverable. What email should I send it to, and when should I follow up after you have looked at it?

## Qualification score

Give one point for each:

- decision maker engaged
- Arizona assisted-living facility
- clear survey/readiness pain
- current process is fragmented/manual
- willingness to show facility/evidence within scope
- ability to pay $299 without long procurement
- potential recurring need
- multi-location expansion potential

**6–8:** high priority  
**4–5:** nurture  
**0–3:** low priority

## Rep workflow

Every qualified lead must have:

- facility/operator name
- contact name and role
- phone/email
- number of locations
- current readiness method
- main pain point
- next action and date
- pilot status
- payment status
- recurring-conversion status

No lead should remain in the pipeline without a next action.

## First 80-target scorecard

Target:

- 80 carefully selected facilities/operators
- 15+ meaningful conversations
- 6+ demos/fit calls
- 3+ paid pilots
- ≥50% pilot-to-recurring interest/conversion

If fewer than 5 serious meetings result from 100 genuinely targeted facilities, revise the message, list quality or offer before adding sales headcount.

## Sales compensation guardrails

For an independent referral/business-development partner, test compensation based on **collected revenue**, subject to proper worker-classification review.

Planning hypothesis:

- 8–10% of collected one-time pilot/service revenue
- 10–15% of collected first-year subscription revenue

Do not pay more for:

- more severe findings
- frightening customers
- promising fines will be avoided
- misrepresenting the service

Use commission clawbacks/refund adjustments for material misrepresentation or canceled/refunded sales.

## When to hire sales

### Before 3 paid pilots

Founder learns the pitch. A helper may set appointments, but do not build a salaried sales team.

### After 3 paid pilots

Test one business-development partner using the proven talk track.

### After repeatable conversion

Only add a second rep when the first channel produces repeatable collected revenue and fulfillment capacity exists.

## Sales-to-operations handoff

A sale is not complete until operations receives:

- paid order/payment confirmation
- facility identity
- customer decision maker
- desired delivery mode: DIY or field-supported
- scope/areas authorized
- privacy/data restrictions
- requested appointment window
- known concerns
- report recipient

Operations then creates the facility binder and schedules fulfillment.

## Ethical rule

Ready Set wins by making the customer's readiness state clearer and more actionable—not by making the customer more afraid.
