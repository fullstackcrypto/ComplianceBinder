# Ready Set Solutions — Field Readiness Specialist SOP

## Purpose

This SOP standardizes a private inspection-readiness walkthrough for Arizona assisted-living facilities. It is designed so trained Ready Set personnel can produce consistent, evidence-based results without the founder being onsite.

## Role title

Use:

- **Ready Set Readiness Specialist**
- **Compliance Readiness Specialist**
- **Field Readiness Specialist**

Do not market the role as an ADHS inspector, certifier, regulator or legal professional.

## Required opening statement

Before beginning the walkthrough, tell the customer:

> Ready Set Solutions is an independent readiness and documentation service. This is not an ADHS inspection, legal opinion or certification, and Ready Set does not guarantee any regulatory outcome. The facility remains responsible for determining and maintaining compliance.

## Pre-visit checklist

Before travel:

1. Confirm facility name, address, contact and appointment window.
2. Confirm the authorized areas and scope of the walkthrough.
3. Confirm whether residents will be present and establish privacy controls.
4. Create/select the facility binder and seed the Assisted Living readiness workflow.
5. Charge phone/tablet and backup battery.
6. Confirm camera and document-upload functions work.
7. Bring approved measuring equipment if physical dimensions may matter.
8. Review any known facility concerns supplied by management.
9. Confirm no resident clinical records or PHI are intentionally requested for the initial pilot.
10. Confirm the customer understands that official requirements control over any Ready Set summary.

## Field privacy rules

### Never capture unless specifically authorized under an approved data-handling scope

- residents' faces
- resident names
- medication labels
- medication administration records
- medical charts
- diagnoses
- Social Security numbers
- financial information
- employee identity documents
- background-check details beyond what is required for the agreed scope

For the initial pilot, prefer facility-level evidence and de-identified indexes.

If sensitive information appears unintentionally in a photo, stop and retake the photo without the sensitive information whenever possible.

## Walkthrough order

Use the same sequence for every facility unless the site requires a safer route.

### 1. Exterior and approach

Review observable conditions such as:

- access paths
- parking/arrival areas within scope
- exterior lighting
- trip/fall hazards
- damaged walking surfaces
- entrance/exit accessibility
- blocked or questionable egress conditions
- exterior maintenance issues

### 2. Entrances, exits and egress

Review:

- doors and hardware
- clear paths
- visible obstruction
- lighting
- signage within scope
- obvious damage
- questionable dimensions requiring measurement verification

### 3. Common areas

Review observable:

- trip/fall hazards
- damaged furnishings
- flooring conditions
- electrical/cord concerns
- lighting
- sanitation/maintenance concerns
- life-safety readiness conditions
- blocked access routes

### 4. Resident rooms within authorized scope

Review physical/environmental conditions only. Do not inspect private resident information.

Potential evidence categories:

- damage
- maintenance needs
- access/clearance concerns
- lighting
- electrical conditions
- trip/fall concerns
- furnishing condition
- physical conditions requiring management review

### 5. Bathroom and bathing areas

Review observable:

- maintenance
- wet/slip risk
- fixture condition
- support/grab features within scope
- hot-water/scald-risk indicators within scope
- access/clearance conditions
- sanitation conditions

### 6. Emergency and life-safety readiness evidence

Ask management to show where authorized records are maintained for items such as:

- emergency/evacuation plan
- evacuation maps
- drills
- inspection records
- corrective actions
- emergency contacts

The specialist records whether evidence is organized/retrievable; the specialist does not invent missing documentation.

### 7. Staff/document readiness index

Within the authorized pilot scope, verify that management can locate evidence for:

- facility license
- manager certification verification
- staff credential/training tracking
- personnel-file/background-check readiness
- policies/procedures
- incident/corrective-action tracking
- maintenance records

Do not upload sensitive source records unless the deployment and customer agreement explicitly permit them.

## Finding format

Every material observation should be written using the same structure:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Location | Specific room/area |
| Condition | Objective observation, not an accusation |
| Priority | Red / Orange / Yellow / Green |
| Evidence | Photo/document/measurement/note |
| Regulatory reference | Current verified citation or `Needs Review` |
| Plain-language reason | Why management should review it |
| Corrective action | Practical next step, not legal advice |
| Owner | Customer person/role responsible |
| Due date | Agreed date |
| Verification | Open / Verification Required / Resolved |

## Priority system

### RED — Immediate management attention

Use only when an observed condition appears to create a serious immediate safety/readiness concern or requires urgent escalation. Do not state that a legal violation has been conclusively established unless a qualified reviewer has verified the applicable authority and evidence.

### ORANGE — Correct soon

Material readiness gap that should be assigned and corrected promptly.

### YELLOW — Improvement / documentation gap

A condition that should be organized, clarified, documented or improved.

### GREEN — Verified/acceptable for the Ready Set scope

Evidence reviewed and no readiness action identified within the limited scope. Green does not mean the facility is officially certified compliant.

## Measurement protocol

### Current launch rule

LiDAR/computer vision is **not required** for the pilot and may not produce final automatic regulatory pass/fail decisions.

If a dimension could affect the final finding:

1. Record the approximate/screening measurement if useful.
2. If the result is close enough to a threshold that measurement error could change the conclusion, mark **Verification Required**.
3. Verify with the approved physical/laser measuring procedure.
4. Record the verified value, instrument, date and specialist.
5. Attach a privacy-safe evidence photo when appropriate.
6. Verify the applicable regulatory threshold before the final report.

Never write `Violation confirmed` based solely on a mobile LiDAR estimate.

Preferred wording:

> Potential dimensional discrepancy identified — verified measurement and regulatory-reference review required.

## Evidence standards

A useful evidence photo should:

- clearly show the condition
- include enough surrounding context to identify location
- avoid residents and sensitive information
- not be misleadingly cropped
- be paired with a short factual note

Do not alter evidence to make a condition appear better or worse.

## Specialist conduct

The specialist must:

- remain neutral and professional
- avoid fear-based statements about fines
- avoid arguing with management
- distinguish observation from interpretation
- avoid promising that a correction guarantees a successful survey
- escalate uncertain regulatory questions for content QA
- avoid accepting cash or side agreements outside the company workflow

## End-of-visit closeout

Before leaving:

1. Confirm all major areas in scope were reviewed.
2. Confirm evidence uploads are complete or queued.
3. Give management a short verbal summary of the highest-priority readiness items.
4. Do not provide a final legal/compliance conclusion onsite.
5. Confirm who will receive the final report.
6. Confirm open corrective-action owners and due dates when possible.
7. Tell the customer the report is subject to Ready Set QA and regulatory-reference verification.

## Post-visit workflow

Within the same business day when possible:

1. Sync/upload all permitted evidence.
2. Remove or replace evidence containing unnecessary sensitive information.
3. Complete finding notes.
4. Mark uncertain citations `Needs Review`.
5. Submit the field package for report QA.

Target final-report delivery: **within 24 hours after the field package and required source verification are complete.**

## Report QA stop-ship checklist

Do not release the report if:

- a critical citation is unverified
- evidence and finding do not match
- a sensitive-data exposure remains in the report/evidence
- measurement verification is incomplete where legally material
- the report uses language implying ADHS affiliation/certification
- the disclaimer is missing

## Required report disclaimer

> Ready Set Solutions is an independent compliance-readiness, documentation and workflow service. Ready Set Solutions is not the Arizona Department of Health Services, is not affiliated with or endorsed by ADHS, and does not conduct official regulatory inspections. Ready Set Solutions does not provide legal advice and does not warrant or guarantee regulatory compliance, licensure, a deficiency-free survey, avoidance of enforcement action, or any particular inspection outcome. Findings reflect information observed or provided as of the assessment date. Facility owners and licensees remain responsible for verifying applicable requirements and maintaining compliance.

## Compensation ethics

Readiness Specialists must never be compensated according to the number or severity of findings. Compensation should be based on completed, quality-reviewed engagements or a properly structured employment arrangement.

This removes any incentive to exaggerate conditions.
